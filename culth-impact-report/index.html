A message
from Heather
and Nicole

Contemplating the impact The Cultch has had on the world this past year has led us to ask the question, “what would the world be like without The Cultch?” We realize that we could come up with a list of tangibles like tens of thousands of people would not have seen the Panto or that hundreds of youth would not have had their first opportunity to perform in front of an audience, however that is not what comes to mind.

What we first see are the faces, the smiles and the laughs, the tears being covertly wiped away, elders side by side with youth, heads bent together in earnest conversation, people holding hands, hugging, shaking their heads in contemplation. It makes us realize that the journey is where the impact happens. Time together is the most valuable thing we can spend. To us, that is the irreplaceable impact The Cultch makes.

Heather Redfern

Executive Director

Nicole McLuckie

Associate Executive Director

    Indigenous Programming

    The Cultch is once again grateful to the Ronald S. Roadburg Foundation for their support of Indigenous Programming. The Cultch presented four productions with their support and provided thousands of tickets through our Indigenous ticket program as well as hosting a variety of welcome events and feasts throughout the season.

    Cultch Connects

    Cultch Connects is our ticket donation program (funded by generous individual donors and the Ronald S. Roadburg Foundation) that allows us to give complimentary tickets to community organizations. The Cultch Connects Program provides a special night out for families and individuals that otherwise could not attend.

Does the street have its tree?

In 1887, CPR surveyor Lauchlan Hamilton mapped this run of streets south of False Creek and named them after trees, meaning to keep them alphabetical. In a 1936 letter he told city archivist Major J.S. Matthews he’d tried to preserve “the arrangement of the alphabet” — Matthews figured Hamilton had handed the list to a draughtsman without saying so, and it landed however it landed. One casualty: the original Cedar Street was renamed Burrard in 1938. Cedar Crescent, tracked below, was named after it in 1913 — and not one of its street trees today is a cedar.

Elizabeth Walker, Street Names of Vancouver (Vancouver Historical Society, 1999), quoting Hamilton’s 1936 letter to Matthews. Source ↗
